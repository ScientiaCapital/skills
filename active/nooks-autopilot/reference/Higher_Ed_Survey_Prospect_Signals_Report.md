## GTME Lens

- **GTM Motion:** Outbound + intent-led ABM. The survey is a self-reported intent dataset — every "Windows pain Yes" or "Matrox installed" is a qualified disqualification of the status quo. That converts a generic Higher Ed prospecting motion into a **trigger-event motion** (vendor exit, platform discontinuation, OS-level breakage).
- **Operational Leverage:** This single CSV pair contains 36 P0 + 71 P1 prospects already qualified against MEDDIC's "Identify Pain" — saving ~4-6 hours of manual research per account. The bucketing logic is reusable; if Marketing runs this survey annually, the same Python classifier reruns in seconds.
- **First-principles question this surfaces:** *Why does Marketing collect this data and then hand it to BDRs as a flat CSV?* The buckets and signals should arrive in HubSpot pre-tagged, so the BDR's job becomes "act on the signal" not "find the signal." This is an automation/Ops project worth pitching to Ashley (DemandGen) as a portfolio artifact.
