## 2. Killer-questions discovery framework (use these, per vertical)
3 steps: (1) find organizational pain, (2) need behind the need, (3) what if things were better. One primary + 4 alternates per step. Pick 1-2 per call, never all five. Tip lines are coaching notes — never read aloud. After step 3: "Can I try a few ideas on you?" then surface ONE Epiphan capability that fits the stated pain. If pain + urgency + future state agreed → book the AE on the call.

### Higher Education (ICP 90)
1. "What's the biggest challenge with lecture capture and classroom recording right now that, if it's still broken a year from now, would really hurt faculty adoption or student access?"
2. "What's happening on campus that's making reliable capture across all your rooms such a priority this year?"
3. "If every room just recorded and published to your CMS or LMS without IT touching it, what would that free your AV team up to do?"

### Live Events (ICP 85)
1. "What's the biggest risk in your live production workflow right now that, if it went wrong on a major show, would really damage the client relationship or your reputation?"
2. "What's driving the pressure to make every stream rock-solid right now?"
3. "If you never had to worry about a dropped feed or a failed multi-camera switch mid-event, what bigger work could your team take on?"

### Hard lessons baked into ranking + discovery:
- The title that converts is **Manager / Director of AV / Media Services / Classroom Technology / Instructional Technology** (ATL). Best progression in the book (44%).
- **Never run a deal on a single Specialist/Engineer thread.** Every 2026 loss (UMD, SANS, Rice, Deep Cove, Waterloo) was single-threaded below Manager. Every advance had Manager-or-above engaged.
- BTL coordinators/technologists book demos but stall at Discovery alone (13/15 of Tim's parked there) — include them, but never as the only contact; thread up to the economic buyer.
- Missing ATL layer to add on every account: **Director of Online / Digital / Academic Technology (the Panopto admin owner)** — fixes the Discovery-stage stall.
- #1 buying trigger in Higher Ed: **legacy capture appliance EOL** — "What's your MediaSite / Seneca / Echo360 exit plan?" (Cornell, NYU Stern, UF Levin, UMB-32, UC Berkeley all came from this).
- EC20 PTZ = the new-logo wedge into Panopto schools (direct-to-Panopto, no Pearl required); came up on 55+ AE calls.

## 5. Product spec quick-reference (verified)
- Pearl Nexus: 1RU, up to 3x 1080p30 channels; Panopto/Kaltura/YuJa remote recorder + user auth; NDI|HX in (NOT full NDI); Edge-managed.
- Pearl-2: up to 6 simultaneous 1080p30 channels (live-switch up to 5); SRT + full NDI in; multi-stream 50+ endpoints.
- Pearl Mini: 3 channels, 2 HDMI + 1 SDI + USB + NDI|HX.
- Pearl Nano: single-channel, HDMI or SDI.
- EC20 PTZ: 4K AI auto-tracking; world's-first Panopto-certified PTZ, direct-to-Panopto, no encoder needed; NDI|HX3, SRT, HDMI 2.0, SDI; Edge-managed.
- Epiphan Edge: fleet management (free monitoring tier; premium = batch ops, alerts, config presets, remote login).
- Epiphan Connect: extracts Teams/Zoom feeds via SRT into Pearl/Unify workflows.
