## Core inputs (Higher Ed / Community College)

### Equipment age
- **Killer Q:** "How old are the rooms that give you the most grief?"
- **Label:** "Sounds like the older rooms are the ones eating your week."
- **Job:** rooms that just record reliably, without babysitting.
- **Insight:** "We typically see gear past five years generate roughly triple the tickets."

### AV-equipped rooms
- **Killer Q:** "How many rooms are you actually responsible for?"
- **Label:** "It seems like the room count grew faster than the team did."
- **Job:** cover every room without adding headcount.
- **Insight:** "Most teams run ~43 rooms per tech — the ones who break that are cloud-managed."

### Peak concurrency  *(the cheaper-path lever — handle with care)*
- **Killer Q:** "Of all those rooms, how many are ever recording at the exact same minute?"
- **Label:** "It sounds like nowhere near all of them are ever live at once."
- **Job:** stop paying for capacity you never use at the same time.
- **Insight:** "Most campuses buy a box for every room — but you only ever record a fraction at once.
  There may be a more affordable path." *(Tease only. Never explain the mechanism. Hand to AE.)*

### Failed / missed captures
- **Killer Q:** "When a recording fails, who finds out first — you, or the professor?"
- **Label:** "It seems like a failed capture isn't a tech ticket, it's a faculty-trust problem."
- **Job:** never explain to a dean why a class wasn't recorded.
- **Insight:** "EC20 publishes straight to your CMS — no encoder in the path to fail."

### Classroom downtime
- **Killer Q:** "When a room drops mid-class, what does it actually take to get it back?"
- **Label:** "Sounds like a downed room is a fire drill, not a ticket."
- **Job:** a room that never takes a class hostage.
- **Insight:** "Cloud-managed fleets catch most of this before the class even notices."

### Support tickets
- **Killer Q:** "In a normal week, how much of your team's time is just AV firefighting?"
- **Label:** "It sounds like the tickets never actually stop."
- **Insight:** "We typically see ~4.6 tickets per room per year on aging rooms — and near zero when
  config is centralized and free in Edge."

### Staff / FTE
- **Killer Q:** "How many rooms is each of your techs covering today?"
- **Job:** redeploy people off babysitting and onto real work.
- **Insight:** "NC State runs 300-plus rooms with a core team of three — the difference is fleet management."

## Vertical riffs (swap the spine, keep it tight)

### Community College (budget-first, aging) — lead with the *price*, not the pain
- **Killer Q:** "If you could cover the campus for a fraction of a box-in-every-room budget, who'd need
  to say yes?"
- **Insight:** "EC20 straight to your CMS means no encoder spend at all — the lowest way in."

### Broadcast (greenfield, post-NAB) — speed & mobilization
- **Killer Q:** "When you fly a kit to a site, how long from case-open to first frame?"
- **Label:** "Sounds like setup time is the thing that actually costs you."
- **Job:** land, power up, go live — no operator wrangling.
- **Insight:** "A portable Pearl Mini switches a multi-cam show out of a single case; EC20 needs no operator."

### Live Events (greenfield, per-event)
- **Killer Q:** "What does one botched show actually cost you — the redo, or the client?"
- **Job:** never hand a client a broken recording.
- **Insight:** "Price the risk per event, not per box — that's the number that moves a production lead."

### Corporate (aging or new-HQ)
- **Killer Q:** "When the all-hands drops, how many thousand people just lost the message?"
- **Job:** every employee gets the town hall, live or on-demand, every time.
- **Insight:** "Multiply a failed all-hands by a loaded hourly rate — it dwarfs the gear."
