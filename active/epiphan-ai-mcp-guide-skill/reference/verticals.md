# The 5-Vertical Persona Pack (+ 3 future)

> INTERNAL. Source of truth: `epiphan-cost-calculator/docs/plans/2026-06-20-persona-vertical-map.md`.
> Titles trace to the AE/Tim 2026 Clari + HubSpot persona-intel analysis.

**Spine that holds for every vertical:** 3-tier cost (operational drag → mission/output failure →
institutional exposure) + a solution spine (room-by-room vs a cheaper path). Only the labels, the
unit economics, and the personas change.

**Two framing lenses:** `aging` ("what your current setup quietly costs" — established install base)
or `greenfield` ("cost of getting it wrong / cost per event" — new-build, per-project, fly-kit).

---

## Community College — BLUE OCEAN (lead)
- **Why it's uncontested:** incumbents chase R1s and funded privates; CC buyers opted out on price and
  were never shown a financial case (Tier-2/Tier-3 noncustomers).
- **ATL economic buyer:** Director/Manager of Media Services, Learning Technology, or Distance
  Education. ⚠️ Do NOT lead with central IT — Foothill-De Anza (Dir IT & Infrastructure) demoed → no deal.
- **BTL champion:** AV Coordinator / Media Services Tech / Classroom Support / Distance-Ed
  Technologist / AV Lead (Bow Valley AV Lead → 2 deals).
- **Headline number:** the *price* — "cover your campus for ~$X, not $Y" + payback. Budget framing leads.
- **Two ramps:** (1) **EC20 direct-to-CMS** (no encoder, lowest cost, the new-logo wedge — explainable);
  (2) pooled "more affordable path" (AV matrix — tease only).
- **Framing:** aging, budget-first. **Matrix-tease fit: HIGHEST.** Motion: phased budget (cf. Grand Forks win).

## Higher Education (R1 / funded privates)
- **ATL:** Director of AV/Media Services / IT Ops (incl. Assoc Dir) — on every $40K+ deal; Director/Head
  of Instructional Technology (owns the CMS workflow).
- **BTL:** Manager of Classroom/Media Services Technology (primary, books demos); AV/Media Systems
  Engineer (always pair with a Manager+); Instructional Technologist / Learning Spaces Lead.
- **Headline number:** Annual Cost of Inaction + 3-yr cumulative + payback/ROI — "the number to take
  to whoever signs."
- **How used:** champion self-serves → shares URL/PDF up to the Director → PDF to the board. Multi-thread
  fix for single-threaded losses.
- **Framing:** aging (legacy capture-appliance EOL — generic phrasing only). Matrix fit: medium-high.

## Live Events
- **ATL:** Owner / Director of Production / Technical Director / Director of Operations (production &
  rental houses). Churches: 0 progressed YTD — deprioritize.
- **BTL:** Lead AV Tech / A1–V1 / Production Engineer / freelance TD.
- **Headline number (greenfield/per-event):** cost per botched-or-lost show + redo labor + client
  make-good/churn. ⚠️ NO tuition/students — drivers **not yet calibrated**.
- **Framing:** greenfield/per-event. Matrix fit: high conceptually (pool across stages), per-project.
- **Pipeline:** Disney Music $33.9K won; INVNT/The Volume/Deep Cove lost → exploratory.

## Corporate
- **ATL:** Director of Workplace Technology / Corporate AV / IT; VP Communications; Head of L&D.
- **BTL:** AV/Collaboration Engineer / Event Producer / IT-AV specialist.
- **Headline number:** loaded employee-hours wasted + failed all-hands reach + training/compliance
  completion risk. ⚠️ Drivers **not yet calibrated**.
- **Framing:** aging (room fleet) or greenfield (new HQ). Corporate IT-first stalls (IQVIA/Alstom/
  Chamberlain demoed → no deal). Matrix fit: medium.

## Broadcast — NEW ACTIVE (post-NAB)
- **Why now:** since NAB the demand signal is workflow **automation**, **easier-to-use** gear, and
  easy **mobilization** — fly-kits for fast remote/onsite setup.
- **ATL:** Director of Engineering / Chief Engineer.
- **BTL:** Broadcast engineer / field engineer.
- **Headline number (greenfield/per-project):** dead air + lost spots + make-good + redundancy cost.
- **Product fit:** Pearl Mini (portable switcher), EC20 (PTZ), Epiphan Unify (remote multi-source),
  Pearl Nano. ⚠️ No "fly-kit" doc page exists — it's a positioning angle, not a spec citation.
- **Framing:** greenfield. Long cycle (NewsNation $50K PoC; Disney $33.9K).

## Outbound rule (from the data)
Never run a deal single-threaded below Manager level — every 2026 loss (UMD, SANS, Rice, Deep Cove)
was single-threaded; every advance had Manager-or-above engaged. Open with the BTL champion, thread up
to the ATL economic buyer.
