# Close Plan Generator — Troubleshooting & FAQs

> Extracted from SKILL.md — content preserved verbatim.

**Q: Skill says "EB not identified." What do I do?**
A: Go back to discovery. Ask Champion: "Who signs the PO?" or "Who approves the budget?" If you still can't identify EB, that's your biggest risk and should be this-week action #1.

---

**Q: Confidence score is 58. Should I keep pushing or reset the close date?**
A: Depends on why it's 58. If MEDDIC or momentum is low (not just EB disengagement), reset close date by 30 days and run 2-3 this-week actions to rebuild. If EB is just disengaged, one intro call may jump you to 75+.

---

**Q: Clari only found 1 call. Is that enough data?**
A: Not ideal. If <2 calls in past 14 days, that's an activity momentum risk. Add this-week action: "Schedule product demo or architecture discussion call with Champion + EB."

---

**Q: Should I send the mutual action plan to the prospect before or after the next meeting?**
A: Craft it after your next call/meeting (so you have commitment). Send same day or next morning with: "Per our discussion, here's our mutual action plan. Please confirm your team can meet these dates."

---

**Q: Incumbent says they'll match Epiphan's price. How do I address in close plan?**
A: Update competitive landscape: "Incumbent willing to discount. Epiphan advantage now: [integration, ease of use, support, reference customers]." Escalate to SE if needed for technical win-angle.

---

**Q: Deal is in "Closed Won" but confidence score said 72. Did close plan fail?**
A: No—close plan was diagnostic at the time. Confidence improved (likely from this-week actions). Run close plan again at next deal stage for learning.

---

**Q: How do I use close plan with a renewal/expansion deal?**
A: Same workflow. Stakeholders may differ (new use case = new users/champions). Focus risk register on: account health, expansion objections (feature gaps?), budget availability.

---

**Q: Should I run close plan on every deal or just ones in Negotiation+?**
A: Minimum: Negotiation stage onward. Optional earlier if you want diagnostic at Qualification/Demo. Running weekly from Negotiation → Close is best practice.
