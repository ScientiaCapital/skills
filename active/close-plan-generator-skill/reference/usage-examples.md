## Example 1: Enterprise Higher Ed Deal

**Trigger:** "Close plan for University of Michigan Pearl Nano deployment"

**Inputs:**
- Deal: "UMich - Pearl Nano + Kaltura Integration" | $45K | Negotiation stage | Close target: May 15
- Contacts: AV Director (Champion), Provost (EB, unengaged), IT Manager (User, engaged)
- Clari: 2 calls past 14 days, positive sentiment, objection re: integration timeline

**Output:**
- Stakeholder map flags Provost as unengaged EB → Action: AE schedules Provost call
- Decision process: Budget approval by Provost (2-3 weeks typical) → Risk: delayed EB engagement
- Competitive: Incumbent Extron SmartInput → Battlecard: Kaltura integration, lower cost
- Confidence: 72/100 (EB disengaged, momentum + champion strong, timeline tight)
- This-week actions: 1) Provost intro call, 2) Kaltura integration spec demo to IT Manager, 3) Reference call with similar Higher Ed customer
- Mutual action plan: Shared with Champion, includes Provost budget approval deadline

## Example 3: Competitive Displacement (Courts/Legal)

**Trigger:** "Close strategy for County Courthouse, RTMP streaming"

**Inputs:**
- Deal: "County Courts - Pearl Nexus + SRT" | $75K | Qualification → Discovery bridge | Close target: June 30
- Contacts: IT Director (Champion, engaged), Judge (User, skeptical of tech), Legal Counsel (Blocker, incumbent Teradek advocate)
- Clari: 3 calls past month, judge skeptical re: learning curve, legal counsel pushing Teradek (known vendor)
- Company: County government, 200 staff, SRT/RTMP requirements for secure courtroom streaming
- Incumbent: Teradek, 5-year relationship, perceived risk of switching

**Output:**
- Stakeholder map: Judge = User (skeptical, risk); IT Director = Champion (strong, technical); Legal Counsel = Blocker (incumbent advocate, needs conversion)
- Decision process: Legal approval critical (judicial system risk-averse), IT Director has influence but Judge has veto power
- Competitive: Teradek incumbent, risk = "known vendor" / political comfort. Epiphan angle: superior SRT reliability, RTMP/S compliance, cost
- Confidence: 62/100 (incumbent threat, user skeptical, legal blocker active; but champion strong + clear technical advantage)
- Risk register: 1) Judge blocks on learning curve → mitigate: simplified UI demo + reference call with similar court, 2) Legal counsel advocates for status quo → mitigate: security brief vs. Teradek + compliance white paper
- This-week actions: 1) IT Director prep call re: judge concerns, 2) Simplified demo for Judge (60 min, IT Director present), 3) Security comparison doc sent to Legal Counsel, 4) Reference call scheduled with similar county
- Mutual action plan: Include judge learning curve mitigation + legal approval timeline
