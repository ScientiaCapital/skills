## Mutual Action Plan (Email-Ready)

Formatted as plaintext or HTML email draft in Gmail with:
- Prospect company name, solution, close target date
- Epiphan commitments (with owner and deadline)
- Prospect commitments (with owner and deadline)
- Open items / risks requiring joint discussion
- Next meeting details
